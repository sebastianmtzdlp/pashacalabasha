Fujifilm C200: the fresh answer to Kodak's warm rolls

Fresh greens, clean skies and a cool palette that's a relief after so much yellow.

By Pasha CalabashaOctober 23, 20262 min read

FUJI 200ISO 200

For years C200 was Fuji's cheap option. It's like a Kodak consumer roll, but from the other side of the spectrum. Where Kodak warms, Fuji cools.

What it looks like

Greens with a touch of mint, clean blues and neutral whites. Skin comes out paler than on Kodak, and shadows lean blue-green.

Where Kodak warms, Fuji cools.

How to expose it

One or two stops more removes the green from the shadows and leaves them bright and pastel.

What we like

  • Fresh, natural palette
  • Good greens
  • Cheap while it was easy to find

Not so much

  • Greenish shadows
  • Harder and harder to find

Who is it for?

For nature, overcast days and anyone who wants a less golden look.

Verdict

A simple film with a lot of personality. In Keepr it's still available, even if shops no longer carry it.
