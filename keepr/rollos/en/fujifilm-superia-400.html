Fujifilm Superia 400: the look of two-thousands snapshots

The Fuji roll everyone carried in their bag. Fresh colour, good contrast, and it behaves well under artificial light.

By Pasha CalabashaOctober 23, 20262 min read

FUJI S-400ISO 400

For a long time, Superia was Fuji's roll for everything. Its 400 was the obvious pick for parties, trips and birthdays.

That's why so many photos from that era carry its colour.

What it looks like

Vivid greens, present magentas and more contrast than C200. Under fluorescent light it does better than many competitors.

So many photos from that era carry its colour.

How to expose it

Fine at ISO 400. Half a stop more cleans up the shadows.

What we like

  • Very versatile
  • Good indoors
  • Fresh colour with contrast

Not so much

  • Visible grain
  • Almost impossible to find today

Who is it for?

For parties, indoor shots and long trips.

Verdict

Pure two-thousands nostalgia. In Keepr it's a good pick for group photos indoors.
