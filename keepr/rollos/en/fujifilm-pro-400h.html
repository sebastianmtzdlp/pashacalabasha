Fujifilm Pro 400H: the pastel that left too soon

Mint greens, airy highlights and the fourth colour layer that made it unique. No longer made; in Keepr it lives on.

By Pasha CalabashaOctober 23, 20262 min read

FUJI 400HISO 400

Pro 400H was the film of outdoor weddings and sessions in front light. Fuji announced its end in 2021, and every roll left has been worth gold since.

Its secret was a fourth colour-sensitive layer that helped it reproduce mixed light and gave it those greens that were all its own.

What it looks like

Fresh and luminous. Greens lean mint, blues lean cyan and skin stays light, almost porcelain.

Overexposed, it turns pastel and ethereal, which is how almost everyone used it.

Every roll left is worth gold.

How to expose it

One or two stops over, always. Underexposed it loses its magic and turns dirty.

What we like

  • Unmistakable pastel
  • Excellent in mixed light
  • Generous latitude

Not so much

  • Discontinued
  • Needs plenty of light

Who is it for?

For weddings, outdoor portraits and anyone who wants a soft, cool look.

Verdict

A film that left a gap. In Keepr it's the fresh counterpart to Portra 400.
