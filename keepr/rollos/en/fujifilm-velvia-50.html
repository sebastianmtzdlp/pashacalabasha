Fujifilm Velvia 50: colour with no apologies

The slide that changed landscape photography. Dense skies, electric greens and no margin for error.

By Pasha CalabashaOctober 23, 20262 min read

FUJI RVPISO 50

When it came out in 1990, Velvia made every other slide look washed out. Landscape photographers adopted it almost at once and never let go.

What it looks like

Very high saturation, hard contrast and deep blacks. Blue skies move toward violet and greens glow.

Because it's a slide, what you see on the light table is the final picture. There's no negative to fix later.

It made every other slide look washed out.

How to expose it

Exact. Half a stop of error already shows. Meter for the highlights and protect them. Many people shoot it at ISO 40.

What we like

  • Intense colour
  • Almost invisible grain
  • Spectacular on the light table

Not so much

  • Minimal latitude
  • Slow: ISO 50
  • Bad for skin

Who is it for?

For landscapes in sun, mountains, sea and sunsets.

Verdict

A classic. In Keepr it makes a grey landscape look like a nineties postcard.
