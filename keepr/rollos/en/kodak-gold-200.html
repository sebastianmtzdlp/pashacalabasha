Kodak Gold 200: a summer afternoon on a roll

ColorPlus's slightly finer cousin. Golden yellows, warm skin, and the feeling that it's always six in the evening.

By Pasha CalabashaOctober 23, 20262 min read

KODAK GB 200-9ISO 200

If ColorPlus is the everyday roll, Gold 200 is the Sunday roll. It has the same warm character as Kodak's consumer negatives, with calmer grain and slightly cleaner colour.

It comes in 35mm and 120. That gives it a second life with medium-format shooters who don't want to pay for Portra.

What it looks like

Golden, just like the box says. Yellows and oranges lead. Greens come out warm and the sky settles into a soft blue. In afternoon light, skin looks healthy and tanned.

It has a bit more saturation and contrast than Portra, but much less edge than Ektar. It's an easy middle ground to like.

Gold 200 makes everything look like six in the evening.

How to expose it

Half a stop or a full stop over suits it. It handles overexposure better than underexposure, like almost all negatives.

What we like

  • Recognisable golden warmth
  • Fine grain for the price
  • Also in 120

Not so much

  • Turns very yellow in cold light
  • Slightly closed shadows

Who is it for?

For low-sun landscapes, trips and casual outdoor portraits.

Verdict

This is the roll I'd recommend for starting with a good one. In Keepr it gives an even gold that works as well on the beach as on the street.
