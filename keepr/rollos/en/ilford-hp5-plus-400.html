Ilford HP5 Plus: black and white that takes a push

Ilford's answer to Tri-X. A bit softer in contrast, just as noble, and famous for handling three stops of push.

By Pasha CalabashaOctober 23, 20262 min read

ILFORD HP5 PLUSISO 400

HP5 Plus is Ilford's black-and-white film for everything. Compared to Tri-X it's a little softer and greyer, with a rounder grain.

What it looks like

Wide midtones, less harsh blacks and even grain. It's easier to print and scan than Tri-X.

Famous for handling three stops of push.

How to expose it

At 400 it stays soft. At 1600 or 3200 it gains contrast and stays usable. That's why it's a favourite for concerts.

What we like

  • Pushes very well
  • Even grain
  • Easy to scan

Not so much

  • Less contrast than Tri-X at its box speed

Who is it for?

For concerts, indoors, the street, and anyone who develops at home.

Verdict

Solid and reliable. In Keepr it's the black and white for when Tri-X feels too hard.
