Kodak UltraMax 400: the all-terrain roll with colour to spare

A stop faster than Gold and much more saturated. The roll that lasts a whole day, from midday sun to late-afternoon shade.

By Pasha CalabashaOctober 23, 20262 min read

KODAK GC 400ISO 400

UltraMax 400 is what Kodak designed for people who wanted one roll for everything. It is faster than ColorPlus and Gold, which shows indoors, on overcast days and with slow-lens cameras.

The trade-off is more grain and much more intense colour. Far from being a problem, that is exactly what makes it fun.

What it looks like

Bright reds, deep blues and full greens. It is warm but less golden than Gold; it leans red more than yellow.

The grain is present without turning dirty, and the contrast is firm. In portraits with soft light, skin stays natural.

If you're only going to carry one roll, make it this one.

How to expose it

At ISO 400 it behaves well; at 200 it's even cleaner. It's a good match for compact cameras with flash.

What we like

  • Versatile in almost any light
  • Intense, cheerful colour
  • Good price

Not so much

  • More visible grain than Gold
  • Can oversaturate reds

Who is it for?

For long trips, parties with flash and compact cameras.

Verdict

An unpretentious roll that rarely fails. In Keepr it's ideal for street sessions with changing light.
