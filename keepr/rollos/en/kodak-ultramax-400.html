Kodak UltraMax 400: the roll for everything, with colour to spare

One stop faster than Gold and much more saturated. It lasts a whole day, from midday sun to late-afternoon shade.

By Pasha CalabashaOctober 23, 20262 min read

KODAK GC 400ISO 400

UltraMax 400 is the roll Kodak made for someone who wants just one roll for everything. It's faster than ColorPlus and Gold. It works indoors, on overcast days and with slow-lens cameras.

The cost is more grain and much more intense colour. That isn't a problem. It's what makes it fun.

What it looks like

Bright reds, deep blues and full greens. It's warm, but less golden than Gold. It leans red more than yellow.

The grain shows, but it doesn't get dirty. Contrast is firm. In portraits with soft light, skin looks natural.

If you're only going to carry one roll, make it this one.

How to expose it

At ISO 400 it behaves well. At 200 it's even cleaner. It's a good choice for compact cameras with flash.

What we like

  • Works in almost any light
  • Intense, cheerful colour
  • Good price

Not so much

  • More grain than Gold
  • Reds can oversaturate

Who is it for?

For long trips, parties with flash and compact cameras.

Verdict

An unpretentious roll that almost never fails. In Keepr it's ideal for the street, where the light keeps changing.
