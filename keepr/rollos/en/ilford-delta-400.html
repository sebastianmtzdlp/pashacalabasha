Ilford Delta 400: fine grain and plenty of sharpness

Ilford's black and white with the finest grain in its family. For people who want detail before character.

By Pasha CalabashaOctober 23, 20262 min read

ILFORD DELTA 400 PROFESSIONALISO 400

Delta 400 has very fine grain for its speed. It's cleaner than HP5 or Tri-X, and you can see it in the shadows and in textures.

It's from the same Ilford family as HP5, but with a different character. Where HP5 gives weight, Delta gives clarity.

What it looks like

Fine, even grain, almost invisible at small sizes. Medium contrast and well-defined blacks.

It handles textures well: stone, leaves, clothes. What would be grain in Tri-X becomes detail here.

Where HP5 gives weight, Delta gives clarity.

How to expose it

At 400 it's the natural choice. Pushed to 800 or 1600 it stays clean, although the grain shows up sooner than on HP5.

What we like

  • Very fine grain
  • Sharpness and detail
  • Good for scanning

Not so much

  • Less character than HP5 or Tri-X
  • Less room to push

Who is it for?

For clean portraits, architecture, black-and-white landscapes, and anyone after detail.

Verdict

If you want black and white without visual noise, this is it. In Keepr it's the clean counterpart to HP5.
